# Loaf: Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Loaf is an iPhone app that helps you stay off your phone. This policy explains what Loaf does with your information. The short version: Loaf does not collect it.

## What Loaf collects

Nothing. Loaf has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. The developer cannot see how you use the app.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your naps, gifts and settings** (session history, daily goal, chosen purpose, language) are stored only in the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **The apps you choose to block.** Loaf uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Your selection is stored on your device as opaque tokens provided by Apple. Loaf cannot read the names of your apps, your usage, or your browsing history, and none of it leaves your device.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device to tell you when a nap ends.

## Purchases

If you buy Loaf Pro, the payment is handled by Apple. Loaf receives only a confirmation that the purchase is valid. Loaf never sees your name, card or Apple Account details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

## Children

Loaf does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
